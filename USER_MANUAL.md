# ISPSC Alumni Connect

## A quick guide for alumni

ISPSC Alumni Connect is a place to look up fellow graduates and rebuild the
connections that usually get lost after graduation. Think of it as the ISPSC
alumni directory with a little bit of a professional network built in.

You do not need to complete everything at once. Create your account first,
then come back later when you have time to add the rest of your details.

## Creating an account

1. Open the Alumni Connect website.
2. Choose **Register now** below the sign-in form.
3. Enter your name, degree, batch year, email address, and a password with at
   least eight characters.
4. Tick the consent box. This allows your profile to appear in the alumni
   directory.
5. Select **Create my profile**.

If the email is already in use, try signing in instead. Each email address can
only have one account.

## Signing in

Enter the email address and password you used when registering, then select
**Sign in**. The dashboard is your starting point after you enter the site.

If you are using a shared computer, remember to use the sign-out icon at the
bottom of the left-hand menu when you are finished.

## Finding classmates and other alumni

There are two easy ways to browse people:

- On the **Dashboard**, the “Recent alumni connections” card shows a few
  people you may know.
- Choose **Directory** in the menu to see the full list.

Use the search box to look for a name, degree, class year, workplace, or
location. For example, typing `2020`, `Information Technology`, or `Vigan`
will narrow the list to matching profiles.

The directory contains real registered members as well as fictional demo
profiles while the service is being tested. The demo profiles have “Sample” in
their names and are not real people.

## Connecting with someone

1. Find the person in the dashboard or directory.
2. Select **Connect** beside their name.
3. The button changes to **Request sent** while you wait.
4. When the other person accepts, the connection appears in **Connections**.

If someone sends *you* a request, open **Connections** and choose **Accept**.
Once accepted, both people see the connection as confirmed.

There is no need to send the same request twice. The site will let you know if
a request already exists.

## Understanding the dashboard

The dashboard is meant to give you a quick picture of the community:

- **Alumni** shows the people currently visible in the directory.
- **Connections** shows your accepted connections.
- **Upcoming events** and **Opportunities** introduce community activities and
  career-related posts.

## Sharing with the community

The rest of the menu is now active too:

- **Messages** lets you send a private note to another directory member.
- **Groups** lets you create a community space or join one that is already
  available.
- **Events** lets members publish an event and RSVP to attend.
- **Opportunities** lets members post a role, project, internship, or
  collaboration and lets others register their interest.
- **Profile** is where you can add your batch, work details, location, and a
  short introduction.
- **Settings** lets you hide your profile from the directory if you prefer to
  keep it private.

Posts, group memberships, RSVP choices, applications, messages, profile
changes, and directory preferences are saved to your account.

## A few good habits

- Use an email address you will continue to check.
- Keep your professional details accurate once profile editing is available.
- Only connect with people you recognise or have a genuine alumni reason to
  contact.
- Do not share your password with anyone, including friends or administrators.

## Need help?

If something does not look right, take a screenshot if possible and include
what you were trying to do. For example: “I searched for my batch, pressed
Connect, and the request did not change.” That makes it much easier for the
Alumni Affairs team to trace the problem.
